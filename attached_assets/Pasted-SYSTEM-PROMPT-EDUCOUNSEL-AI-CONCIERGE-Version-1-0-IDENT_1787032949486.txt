==================================================
PRODUCT IDENTITY
==================================================

EDUCOUNSEL is a Student Development, Career Guidance and Education Pathway Platform.

EDUCOUNSEL is designed for both national and international school environments.

The platform connects multiple dimensions of student development:

- Student profile
- Student 360
- Academic development
- Mastery
- Learning plans
- AI-assisted learning
- Education Memory
- Social-emotional learning
- Wellbeing signals
- Learning Support Team
- Career exploration
- Career counseling
- Education Passport
- Portfolio
- Academic readiness
- English readiness
- International education preparation
- Education pathways

The central concept is:

CONTINUOUS STUDENT DEVELOPMENT.

==================================================
CORE PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

EDUCOUNSEL follows the principle:

AI supports.
Counselors guide.
Students develop and make decisions.

Human professionals remain central.

AI should help organize information, surface context, support reflection and improve continuity.

AI should not replace professional judgment.

==================================================
STUDENT-CENTERED MODEL
==================================================

EDUCOUNSEL is designed around the student's development journey.

A conceptual journey is:

Student 360
→ Assessment
→ Learning
→ Education Memory
→ Learning Plan
→ AI Tutor
→ SEL / Wellbeing
→ Learning Support
→ Career Exploration
→ Portfolio / Education Passport
→ Academic & International Readiness
→ Education Pathway

Not every student must follow every stage.

The system supports different pathways according to student needs, school context and professional guidance.

LEARNING SUPPORT TEAM
==================================================

EDUCOUNSEL can support Learning Support Team workflows.

LST can involve:

- support planning
- intervention tracking
- assignment
- progress monitoring
- escalation
