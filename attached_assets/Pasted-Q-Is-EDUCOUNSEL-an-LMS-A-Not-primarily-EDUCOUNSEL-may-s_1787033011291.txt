Q: Is EDUCOUNSEL an LMS?

A:
Not primarily.

EDUCOUNSEL may support learning-related workflows, but its broader purpose is connecting student development, learning, wellbeing, career, portfolio, readiness and education pathways.

---

Q: Is EDUCOUNSEL an AI counselor?

A:
No.

EDUCOUNSEL uses AI to support students and counselors, but professional counselors remain central to interpretation and decision-making.

---

Q: Is EDUCOUNSEL only for international schools?

A:
No.

EDUCOUNSEL is designed for both national and international school environments.

---

Q: Can a national school use EDUCOUNSEL for international university preparation?

A:
Yes.

A national-school student can use relevant EDUCOUNSEL capabilities to explore international education, subject to the school's implementation and the student's pathway.

---

Q: Can international schools use EDUCOUNSEL?

A:
Yes.

Potential use cases include student development, career counseling, portfolio development, academic readiness, English readiness, standardized-test preparation and international education pathways.

---

Q: Does EDUCOUNSEL replace a career counselor?

A:
No.

EDUCOUNSEL is designed to strengthen counselor work by providing structured context and AI-assisted support.

---

Q: Does EDUCOUNSEL guarantee university admission?

A:
No.

EDUCOUNSEL can support preparation and pathway planning, but admission decisions remain with individual institutions.

---

Q: What is Education Memory?

A:
Education Memory is the contextual layer that allows relevant student-development information to be connected across the EDUCOUNSEL experience.

---

Q: What is Gustafta's role?

A:
Gustafta provides AI/intelligence infrastructure that supports EDUCOUNSEL's AI capabilities.

EDUCOUNSEL remains the education product.

---

Q: What makes EDUCOUNSEL different?

A:
Its focus is not one isolated function.

EDUCOUNSEL is designed to connect learning, wellbeing, learning support, career development, portfolio, readiness and education pathways into a more continuous student-development context.
